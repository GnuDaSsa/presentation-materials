Section 16 · 성남시의 역할
The supplied presentation explains Seongnam’s support before and after MPW.
Before MPW: EDA, hardware emulators, shared equipment and design support.
After MPW: measurement, analysis and debugging to inform design improvements.
The named programs are the System Semiconductor Development Support Center
and shared advanced equipment for fabless companies.

Section 17 · 클러스터 조성
Co-location is explained in terms of reducing the effort of repeated consultation
between fabless firms, technology suppliers, specialist service providers and customers.

Section 18 · 판교 시스템반도체 연구센터
The research-center concept combines space for companies, development support,
shared equipment, and support or collaboration facilities.
Its purpose is to bring companies, equipment, institutions and specialist personnel together.

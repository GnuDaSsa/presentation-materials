# Seongnam: The Heart of the System Semiconductor Cluster

**English Presentation Script — V1.6**  
**Target duration:** Approximately 5 minutes, depending on speaking pace.

## 1. Opening — Introduction

Good afternoon, everyone.

It's a great honor to speak here today.

Thank you for visiting Seongnam as part of your official visit to Korea, representing Vietnam's Ministry of Science and Technology.

Today, I will introduce Seongnam's policies to support the system semiconductor industry.

My name is Jinwoo Sa, and I work for the Semiconductor Team in the AI Semiconductor Division at Seongnam City Hall.

I am also certified as an AI Champion Black, the highest level of the AI Champion certification program in Korea.

In fact, I created today's presentation slides and the live captions you're seeing now myself, using AI technology.

Now, let me begin my presentation, titled **“Seongnam: The Heart of the System Semiconductor Cluster.”**

## 2. Our Vision and Strategy

Our vision is to become a leading city specializing in system semiconductors.

Seongnam has a unique strength.

**About 40 percent of South Korea's fabless semiconductor companies are located here in Seongnam.**

Our goal is to build a strong ecosystem for the system semiconductor industry in Seongnam.

Based on this industrial environment, we have developed three main strategies.

**Infrastructure, talent development, and industrial space.**

Let me explain how we are putting these strategies into action.

## 3. Infrastructure — Development Support

First, infrastructure.

One of our major programs is the System Semiconductor Development Support Center.

This program consists of three major projects.

The first is the **System IC Verification Center**.

The second is a **Semiconductor Testing Project for the Advanced Defense Industry**.

And the third is the **Advanced Equipment Sharing Program for Fabless Companies**.

These projects provide companies with access to advanced equipment, EDA tools, and testing facilities.

We also offer technical training and consulting services.

Through these programs, we aim to reduce development costs and strengthen the competitiveness of local companies.

## 4. Talent Development

Second, talent development.

We operate a three-step semiconductor training program.

The program covers three levels: practical skills, professional skills, and advanced expertise.

Each level is supported by a university.

At Korea Polytechnics Seongnam Campus, students receive training in AI semiconductor manufacturing, packaging, and testing.

Gachon University provides a specialized course in digital chip design.

And Sogang University's Pangyo Campus offers an advanced semiconductor design program.

These programs have two main goals.

**For young people, we provide the skills they need to start their careers.**

**For local companies, we help develop skilled workers right here in Seongnam.**

This is how we connect education directly with the needs of the semiconductor industry.

## 5. Industrial Space — Pangyo Research Center

Third, industrial space.

We are planning to establish the Pangyo System Semiconductor Research Center in the Third Pangyo Techno Valley.

The center is scheduled for completion in 2030.

It will provide office and research space for semiconductor companies.

What makes this project special is that the first three floors will house the System Semiconductor Development Support Center I mentioned earlier.

This means companies in the building will have convenient access to shared equipment, technical support, and other services.

**Our goal is not simply to provide office space, but to create an environment where companies can develop technologies, collaborate, and grow together.**

## 6. Closing

Seongnam is committed to the sustainable growth of the system semiconductor industry.

Through these three strategies, we aim to strengthen the competitiveness of fabless companies, create high-quality jobs, and build a strong industrial ecosystem.

Ultimately, we hope to establish Seongnam as a key hub in South Korea's semiconductor industry.

This concludes my presentation on Seongnam's semiconductor policies and programs.

Thank you very much for your attention.
